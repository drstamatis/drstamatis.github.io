Speaking

Keynotes, panels &
workshops

I speak to health-tech and AI companies, clinical and academic audiences, and workplaces about what it really takes to build — and trust — technology that touches people's mental health.

On stage
Signature talks

Talks I'm known for.

  • 01
    Engaging, Safe, Effective: Scaling AI for Mental Health

    The hard part isn't building an AI mental-health product — it's building one people actually use that stays safe and clinically sound as it scales. Engagement and safety get treated as opposites; the best products refuse that trade-off. What it takes to design for both, drawn from leading research at Slingshot AI and a study of tens of thousands of real conversations.

  • 02
    High-Functioning, Running on Empty

    Mental health for high performers in tech, medicine, and academia — why the most capable people are often the most quietly depleted, why they're the least likely to ask for help, and what organizations can do about it.

  • 03
    Building a Research Function That Ships

    For founders and leaders standing up or scaling a research function: how to build an evidence strategy that earns the trust of buyers, payers, and regulators and sharpens the product — what evidence you actually need at each stage, when to invest, and how to build a team that drives the roadmap instead of just publishing papers.

Every talk is tailored to the audience — I'll build the version that fits your room, whether that's an engineering team, a clinical conference, a boardroom, or a lecture hall.

Consulting

For companies building digital health products who want to get the clinical side right — not as an afterthought, but as a core part of how they build.

Therapy

Individual
Therapy

I work with adults navigating anxiety, depression, perfectionism, burnout, life transitions, and the particular brand of stress that comes with high-performance environments.

My approach is grounded in evidence-based frameworks — primarily cognitive behavioral therapy and acceptance-based approaches — but what that looks like in practice is a conversation tailored to you, not a protocol applied at you.

I'm especially drawn to working with people in tech, healthcare, and academia — partly because I understand those worlds from the inside, and partly because the pressures of those environments create a specific kind of struggle that benefits from a therapist who gets the context.

Therapy is offered virtually. I am licensed in New York, New Jersey, and all PSYPACT states.

Therapy

Individual
Therapy

I work with adults navigating anxiety, depression, perfectionism, burnout, life transitions, and the particular brand of stress that comes with high-performance environments.

My approach is grounded in evidence-based frameworks — primarily cognitive behavioral therapy and acceptance-based approaches — but what that looks like in practice is a conversation tailored to you, not a protocol applied at you.

I'm especially drawn to working with people in tech, healthcare, and academia — partly because I understand those worlds from the inside, and partly because the pressures of those environments create a specific kind of struggle that benefits from a therapist who gets the context.

Therapy is offered virtually. I am licensed in New York, New Jersey, and all PSYPACT states.

FAQ

Common questions

Who is Caitlin Stamatis?

Caitlin Stamatis, PhD is a licensed clinical psychologist and digital-health researcher. She has led research at Slingshot AI, Google, Akili Interactive, and Otsuka, holds a PhD from the University of Miami, completed residency at Weill Cornell Medicine, and advises health-tech companies on clinical strategy and evidence.

What does the health-tech consulting include?

Clinical strategy, research and outcomes design, content and safety review, and team workshops for companies building digital health and AI mental health products. Engagements range from one-time reviews to ongoing advisory relationships.

What kinds of companies does she work with?

Early-stage startups, growth-stage companies, and established organizations building digital therapeutics, AI mental health tools, and other products that touch people's mental health.

Does she see therapy clients, and in what states?

Yes. She provides virtual individual therapy and is licensed in New York and New Jersey, and through PSYPACT can see clients in the 40+ U.S. states that participate in the PSYPACT telehealth compact.

What does she treat in therapy?

Adults navigating anxiety, depression, perfectionism, burnout, and life transitions, with a focus on high-performers in tech, medicine, and academia. Her approach draws primarily on cognitive behavioral and acceptance-based therapies.

What topics does she speak on, and how do I book her?

AI and mental health, digital therapeutics, clinical evidence in health tech, and workplace mental health. Speaking and panel requests can be sent through the contact page.
